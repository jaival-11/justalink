Create Your Link-in-Bio

Everything is stored inside your shareable link. No signups, no databases.

1. Profile Details

2. Choose Theme Style (Flat Solid Colors)

Select a flat, high-contrast palette. Gradients and blurs are disabled by design philosophy.

3. Social Icons

Add social links as icons displayed in a row after your bio. Enter icon names from simpleicons.org (e.g. github, twitter, instagram, linkedin).

4. Short Message

Add a brief single-line announcement or tagline displayed after social buttons and before your links.

5. Add Links

6. Your Generated Link

Copy this link to share it on social media. Your data is compressed and encoded in the URL. You can also use this same URL to restore and edit your profile anytime using the Restore button at the top.
